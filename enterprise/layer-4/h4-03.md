## H4-03 — Enterprise Production Knowledge Can Become AI-Ready

> **Hypothesis:** The enterprise's accumulated production knowledge can be structured in a form that allows AI to increasingly understand, reason about and act upon it.

### Summary

Blueprints, domain models, configuration, policies and generated systems create explicit relationships between business requirements and production outcomes.

### Implementation

Enterprise Knowledge → Structured Models + Blueprints → Production Context → AI-Accessible Knowledge → AI Action.

### Value — Today

Production knowledge becomes more explicit, discoverable and reusable.

### Value — Tomorrow

AI can increasingly use the enterprise production knowledge base to recommend, generate and improve software systems.

### Evidence

[Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints); [Domain Models](../../evidence/domain-models); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems)

### Vision

The enterprise possesses a growing body of production knowledge that is both executable by Harbormaster and increasingly understandable by AI.
