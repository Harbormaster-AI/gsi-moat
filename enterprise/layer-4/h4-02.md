## H4-02 — AI Can Progress From Assisting To Authoring

> **Hypothesis:** As enterprise production knowledge becomes more structured and executable, AI can progress from assisting developers to creating increasingly complete production artifacts.

### Summary

AI can initially assist with production tasks and potentially progress toward authoring models, configurations, blueprint components and systems.

### Implementation

AI Assistance → AI-Generated Production Artifacts → Validation → AI-Assisted Production → Increasingly Autonomous Authoring.

### Value — Today

AI can assist with defined software-production activities using structured production context.

### Value — Tomorrow

AI may increasingly author portions of the production model and generated system under enterprise controls.

### Evidence

[Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints/README.md); [Domain Models](../../evidence/domain-models/README.md); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems/README.md)

### Vision

AI progresses from a development assistant toward an increasingly capable author of enterprise software-production artifacts.
