## H4-01 — Structured Production Knowledge Makes AI More Valuable

> **Hypothesis:** AI can become more useful when it operates against structured, validated organizational production knowledge rather than relying primarily on generic code-generation context.

### Summary

Blueprints, domain models, configurations and generated systems provide structured context about how systems are intended to be built.

### Implementation

Expose machine-readable production artifacts as the context in which AI assists, recommends and verifies.

### Value — Today

AI can assist around structured blueprints and models.

### Value — Tomorrow

AI can operate against an increasingly rich body of validated organizational knowledge.

### Evidence

[Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints/README.md); [Domain Models](../../evidence/domain-models/README.md); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems/README.md)

### Vision

The GSI's accumulated production knowledge becomes a high-value context for AI-driven software production.
