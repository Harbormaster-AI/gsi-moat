## H2-04 — Industry Knowledge Can Become Reusable Production IP

> **Hypothesis:** Industry knowledge can be represented as domain models that directly participate in producing systems.

### Summary

Domain models provide a structured representation of business concepts and relationships that can be reused across systems.

### Implementation

Combine industry domain models with technology and solution blueprints.

### Value — Today

Existing domain models provide reusable industry context.

### Value — Tomorrow

Project experience can continuously deepen and refine domain models.
