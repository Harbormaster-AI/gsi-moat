## H4-02 — AI Can Progress From Assisting to Authoring

> **Hypothesis:** As structured production knowledge grows, AI can progress from assisting developers and operators to authoring increasingly complete production artifacts.

### Summary

AI can initially assist with blueprint creation, configuration and analysis and progressively take responsibility for generating and extending production knowledge.

### Implementation

AI Assistance → Blueprint Extension → Blueprint Authoring → System Generation → Production Automation.

### Value — Today

AI can assist with the creation and refinement of production knowledge.

### Value — Tomorrow

AI can increasingly author production artifacts within defined models, blueprints, policies and validation boundaries.

### Evidence

[AI-Authored Models](../../evidence/ai-authored-models); [Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints/README.md); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems/README.md)

### Vision

AI evolves from a development assistant into an increasingly capable author of executable production knowledge.
