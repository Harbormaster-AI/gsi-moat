## H4-03 — Production and Operational Knowledge Can Become AI-Ready

> **Hypothesis:** The same structured knowledge used to produce systems can provide a foundation for AI-assisted deployment, management and operations.

### Summary

When application, infrastructure and operational knowledge are represented consistently, the resulting corpus can be used across both production and operational workflows.

### Implementation

Production Knowledge + Operational Knowledge → Unified Structured Corpus → AI-Assisted Production and Operations.

### Value — Today

Production artifacts provide explicit information about intended system architecture, infrastructure and deployment requirements.

### Value — Tomorrow

Operational information can be connected to the production knowledge that created the workload, improving AI's ability to reason across the system lifecycle.

### Evidence

[Production Knowledge](../../evidence/production-knowledge); [Infrastructure](../../evidence/infrastructure); [Operations](../../evidence/operations)

### Vision

The knowledge used to create a workload becomes part of the knowledge used to understand and operate it.
