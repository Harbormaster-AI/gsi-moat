## H4-05 — ISVs Will Drive Software Production by Creating Blueprints to Accelerate Adoption and Usage of Their Technology

> **Hypothesis:** Independent software vendors can use Harbormaster blueprints to encode the production knowledge required to incorporate their technologies into software systems, making those technologies easier to adopt and increasing their potential usage.

### Summary

An ISV can create and maintain blueprints that make its technology a reusable component of software-system production rather than requiring each development team to independently learn and implement its capabilities.

### Implementation

ISV Technology → Production Blueprint → Generated System → Technology Adoption → Technology Usage.

### Value — Today

A technology can be represented within a blueprint and incorporated into generated systems using defined production patterns.

### Value — Tomorrow

ISVs can expand blueprint coverage around their technologies, reducing adoption effort and making their capabilities available across a broader range of software-production events.

### Evidence

[Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints/README.md); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems/README.md); [Blueprint Coverage](../../evidence/blueprint-coverage)

### Vision

ISVs become participants in software production by turning their technology expertise into reusable production capability that accelerates adoption and expands usage.
