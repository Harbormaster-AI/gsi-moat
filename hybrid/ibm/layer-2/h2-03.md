## H2-03 — Smaller, Focused Teams Can Deliver Systems That Previously Required Larger Delivery Teams

> **Hypothesis:** As a greater portion of system production becomes executable, fewer people may be required to perform the repeated implementation work necessary to deliver the same scope.

### Summary

Harbormaster moves production effort from manually implementing established capabilities toward defining models, configuring systems and addressing project-specific requirements.

### Implementation

Production Knowledge → Generated Foundation → Focused Engineering → Project-Specific Innovation.

### Value — Today

Generated systems can eliminate defined categories of repetitive implementation work.

### Value — Tomorrow

Increasing blueprint coverage and production automation can allow teams to deliver more system scope without proportionally increasing delivery headcount.

### Evidence

[Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems); [Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints)

### Vision

Delivery organizations can increasingly concentrate engineering talent on differentiated work rather than repeatedly constructing established system capabilities.
