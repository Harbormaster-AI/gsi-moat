## H2-04 — Lower Production Costs Can Improve Both Client Economics and Delivery Margins

> **Hypothesis:** Reducing the production effort required to deliver a system creates economic flexibility that can be allocated between lower client cost, improved delivery margin, increased competitiveness, or some combination.

### Summary

Harbormaster reduces repeated production effort. The resulting economic benefit does not inherently belong to either the client or the delivery organization; its allocation depends on the delivery model.

### Implementation

Less Production Effort → Lower Delivery Cost → Lower Client Price and/or Higher Margin.

### Value — Today

Reduced repeated production effort can create measurable delivery-cost advantages on applicable systems.

### Value — Tomorrow

As production coverage expands, the economic impact can extend across a larger portion of delivery engagements.

### Evidence

[Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems); [Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints); [Delivery Measurements](../../evidence/delivery-measurements)

### Vision

Software-delivery organizations can compete on both economics and capability without requiring proportional increases in production resources.
