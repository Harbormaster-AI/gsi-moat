## H1-04 — Enterprises Can Retain and Reuse Production Knowledge Beyond Individual Projects

> **Hypothesis:** Software-production knowledge can become an organizational asset that survives individual projects, developers and delivery teams.

### Summary

Harbormaster captures production knowledge in persistent blueprints, domain models and system configurations that can be reused across projects.

### Implementation

Project Knowledge → Model & Blueprint → Versioned Production Asset → Reuse Across Projects.

### Value — Today

Organizations can explicitly capture and reuse defined production knowledge.

### Value — Tomorrow

An increasing portion of institutional software-production knowledge can remain available independent of the people who originally applied it.

### Evidence

[Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints); [Domain Models](../../evidence/domain-models); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems)

### Vision

Enterprise software-production knowledge becomes durable organizational capital rather than knowledge that is repeatedly recreated or lost between projects.
