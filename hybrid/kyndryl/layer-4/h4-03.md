## H4-03 — AI Can Increasingly Extend and Author Production Knowledge

> **Hypothesis:** As production knowledge becomes increasingly structured, AI can progress from assisting with software production to extending and eventually authoring portions of the blueprints and models used for production.

### Summary

Structured blueprints, domain models, configurations and generated systems provide AI with a defined production context in which proposed changes can be evaluated against known production rules and outcomes.

### Implementation

Structured Knowledge → AI Assistance → Blueprint Extension → Blueprint Authoring → Validated Production Knowledge.

### Value — Today

AI can assist with structured production knowledge and development tasks where the relevant context is available.

### Value — Tomorrow

AI can increasingly extend existing blueprints and assist in creating new production knowledge, subject to validation and certification.

### Evidence

[Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints); [Domain Models](../../evidence/domain-models); [AI Production Experiments](../../evidence/ai-production)

### Vision

AI increasingly participates in the creation and improvement of the production knowledge from which software systems are generated.
