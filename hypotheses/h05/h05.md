## H05 · LAYER 2 — Technology Expertise Can Become Technology Blueprints

> **Hypothesis:** Expertise in a technology can be encoded as an executable blueprint rather than remaining primarily a collection of certifications, experience and individual knowledge.

### Summary

Blueprints can represent both generic technology capabilities and targeted technology solutions.

### Implementation

Spring Boot provides a generic foundation; Axon Framework 4 adds an event-sourcing solution; Corda derives from Axon 4 and adds distributed-ledger capabilities.

### Value — Today

Technology expertise can be represented in reusable blueprints.

### Value — Tomorrow

A growing portfolio of technology and solution blueprints can cover strategic technologies.

### Vision

Technology partnerships become opportunities to build executable solution capabilities around those technologies.
