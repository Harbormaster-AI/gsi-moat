## H14 · LAYER 4 — A GSI Can Build AI-Ready Engineering Knowledge

> **Hypothesis:** A GSI that systematically captures its engineering knowledge as structured production assets can create an organizational knowledge base that becomes increasingly useful to AI.

### Summary

People, projects, methodologies, technology expertise and domain knowledge can increasingly be represented through reusable production artifacts.

### Implementation

Continuously accumulate validated blueprints, domain models, generated systems, policies and project-derived refinements.

### Value — Today

Engineering knowledge becomes more structured and reusable.

### Value — Tomorrow

The accumulated body of knowledge becomes a progressively richer substrate for AI-driven production.

### Evidence

[Blueprints](../../evidence/blueprints); [Domain Models](../../evidence/domain-models); [Generated Systems](../../evidence/generated-systems)

### Vision

AI increasingly understands and participates in how this organization builds software.
